Keyboard keys

Press Tab until an item has a pink outline. Then:

  • Alt+Up / Alt+Down: previous / next item, from an item or a scope. A collapsed item expands.
  • Down / Up: into the query or scope / out of it. Left / Right: previous / next scope.
  • Enter: on an item, go to the query; on a scope, edit it. Enter then confirms, Escape cancels.
  • T: on an item, go to the title. Escape in the title or query: back to the item.
